About Us

Sawtelle Residents Association

Comprised of both renters and property owners, the Association began as SaveSawtelle, a grassroots effort in response to the West Los Angeles Community Plan Update Draft of 2023 and the Citywide Housing Incentives Program.

Housing was planned for our area contrary to fundamental planning and environmental justice principles that threatened to erase the cultural identity of our neighborhood.

Sawtelle Neighborhood Association helps the community know and understand what is going on with the City’s land use planning and housing inclusivity policies in our area. Sawtelle has a deep history and we LOVE it.

Our goal is not NIMBY-ism; We want to make sure that the Sawtelle community has a sense of ownership and a voice in how the neighborhood grows and expands.

We want to make sure that growth doesnt come with the loss of cultural identity and expansions of inequity.

We are always looking for volunteers and folks in the neighborhood who care about Sawtelle. Please reach out if that's you!

How we work

We meet irregularly based off upcoming neighborhood updates, and otherwise communicate via text and email.
